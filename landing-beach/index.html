The things everyone checks.

Can I cancel?

There's nothing to cancel. You pay month by month — don't renew and it simply ends when the month you've paid for runs out. No contract, no notice period, no exit fee.

Do I have to book ahead?

For pools, the water park, the gym and the range — no, just turn up. Courts are booked by the hour so two people don't arrive for the same slot; members do that from their phone, 6am to 7pm.

How do I pay?

Bitcoin over Lightning — exactly $75. PayPal and on-chain Bitcoin also work; they add the payment processor's fee on top (5% and 2.5%).
